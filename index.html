What is happening?

Preliminary discussions about the possibility of offering accelerated Engineering Studies have taken place with the school. However, before anything can move forward, we need to know whether there is enough genuine student interest.

Expressing interest does not mean that the course will definitely be offered. The final decision rests with the school and depends on factors such as demand, staffing, timetabling and school approval.

⚙️ What is Engineering Studies?

Engineering Studies is a Stage 6 subject that explores engineering concepts, design, materials, mechanics, electronics and engineering applications.

🧠 Why take it?

Engineering Studies can provide an early foundation in engineering and physics-related concepts while developing practical design and problem-solving skills.

🚀 Why accelerate?

Completing the course earlier could provide additional flexibility in Years 11 and 12, such as allowing another subject to be taken or reducing the number of subjects being completed later.

📚 Physics foundation

Engineering Studies includes concepts that overlap with areas of physics, potentially providing useful groundwork for students interested in studying Physics.

Who should consider expressing interest?

Accelerated Engineering Studies is a significant commitment. Students should carefully consider whether they meet the following criteria.

  1. You are not currently taking another accelerated course, or are willing to give one up if required.
  2. You demonstrate academic excellence.
  3. You have the time and commitment required.
  4. If invited to participate in the course, you are expected to accept your place if you have expressed genuine interest.
  5. The course is subject to school approval.
  6. All information provided must be accurate.
  7. Expressing interest or being accepted into the process does not guarantee that the course will be offered.
  8. Student interest information may be visible to other students. Personal contact details will not be publicly displayed.
  9. The final decision regarding the course is made by the school.

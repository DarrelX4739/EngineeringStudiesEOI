What is happening?

Preliminary discussions have taken place with the school about the possibility of offering Engineering Studies in an accelerated pathway.

However, before anything can move forward, there needs to be genuine student interest. This expression-of-interest portal is intended to help demonstrate whether there is enough demand for the school to consider the proposal.

This is not an enrolment form and submitting an expression of interest does not guarantee that the course will be offered. The final decision will be made by the school and will depend on factors such as student demand, staffing, timetabling, resources and school approval.

⚙️ What is Engineering Studies?

Engineering Studies is a Stage 6 subject that introduces students to engineering concepts, engineering mechanics, materials, electronics, design, communication and practical engineering applications.

🧠 Why should you take it?

Engineering Studies can be a strong option for students interested in engineering, physics, design, robotics, manufacturing, technology or other STEM pathways. It develops problem-solving, design and technical thinking skills while exposing students to real engineering concepts.

🚀 Why accelerate it?

Completing an HSC subject earlier can provide additional flexibility later in high school. Depending on the final structure approved by the school, students could potentially have more flexibility in Years 11 and 12, including having one less subject to manage later or using the available space for another subject.

📚 Physics foundations

Engineering Studies includes concepts that can overlap with areas of physics and mechanics. For students considering Physics, engineering or a related university pathway, the subject can provide useful early exposure to technical concepts.

🎓 An additional HSC subject

For students who are academically capable and interested in the subject, completing Engineering Studies as an accelerated course can potentially provide an additional HSC subject without adding another full subject load to Years 11 and 12. The exact arrangements would depend on the school's approved course structure.

⏱️ One less thing later

Accelerating a subject can mean having one less subject to complete during the main HSC years. That can give students more flexibility to focus on their remaining subjects, provided they can successfully manage the accelerated course.

Why should you consider expressing interest?

You should only express interest if you would genuinely consider taking Engineering Studies if the school approves the accelerated pathway.

  • You are interested in engineering, physics, robotics, design, technology or STEM.
  • You want early exposure to engineering concepts.
  • You would value the possibility of completing an HSC subject earlier.
  • You want potentially more flexibility during Years 11 and 12.
  • You are prepared to manage the workload of an accelerated subject.
  • You have strong academic foundations and are willing to commit the required time.

Who should consider expressing interest?

Accelerated Engineering Studies would be a significant commitment. Students should carefully consider the following before submitting an expression of interest.

  1. You are not currently committed to another accelerated course, or understand that timetable/course requirements may require a change.
  2. You have demonstrated strong academic performance.
  3. You have the time and commitment required.
  4. You have genuine interest in Engineering Studies.
  5. You understand that the course is subject to school approval.
  6. All information submitted must be accurate.
  7. Expressing interest does not guarantee that the course will be offered.
  8. The final decision regarding the course is made by the school.

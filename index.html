Interested in Accelerated Engineering Studies?

We're collecting expressions of interest from students who would like the possibility of taking Engineering Studies earlier than normally offered.

Important: This is an expression of interest only. It does not guarantee that Accelerated Engineering Studies will be offered. Any course would still require school approval and depend on factors such as staffing, timetabling and other requirements.

Why are we collecting interest?

The purpose of this form is to demonstrate how many students would be interested in an accelerated Engineering Studies pathway. The more accurate the expression of interest, the better the school can understand potential demand.

Conditions

1. Expression of interest

Submitting this form indicates that you are interested in the possibility of taking Accelerated Engineering Studies.

2. No guarantee

Submitting an expression of interest does not guarantee that the course will be introduced or that you will be able to enrol in it.

3. School approval

Any accelerated Engineering Studies course would be subject to the relevant school approval, staffing, timetable and course requirements.

4. Accurate information

You agree that the information you provide is accurate and that you are submitting the form yourself.

5. Use of information

The information collected through this form is intended to determine student interest and assist with demonstrating potential demand for the course.

6. Visibility

Your name and year may appear on a list showing students who have expressed interest. Your email address will not be publicly displayed.

7. Withdrawal

If you change your mind, contact the person responsible for managing the expression of interest list.

8. Final decision

The final decision regarding whether accelerated Engineering Studies can occur rests with the school and relevant decision-makers.
